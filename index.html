Questions

Before you order

Why only dark roast?

Dark roast spends more time in the drum, drawing out a bolder, deeper flavor with less acidity. It's the roast for people who want the cup to hit hard and taste like it.

Do you sell ground coffee?

No, and we won't. Grinding is the one step we think you should do yourself, right before you brew.

Where do the beans come from?

Two origins: Ethiopian and Costa Rican. Both dark roast, both whole bean, both 12oz bags.

Do you offer a subscription?

Not yet — for now every order is one-time. Join the list below and you'll be the first to know when subscriptions go live.
