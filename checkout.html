For the person wiring this up

This page is the front-end only — it doesn't move money yet. The recommended way to activate it is Stripe Checkout: create a Payment Link or Checkout Session server-side, then point the "Pay" button at it. Stripe hosts the actual card entry, so you never touch or store raw card numbers here, which keeps you out of PCI-DSS scope.

Where the wiring goes: replace the checkoutForm submit handler in the script below with a call to your backend endpoint (e.g. /create-checkout-session), which creates the Stripe session and redirects to session.url. PayPal and Apple Pay tabs are placeholders for their respective SDKs.

Say the word and I can write that backend endpoint (Node/Express or a serverless function) and the exact Stripe.js snippet to drop in here.
